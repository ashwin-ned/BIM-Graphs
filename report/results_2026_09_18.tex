\begin{abstract}
\noindent
This document consolidates every quantitative result the project currently
stands behind, and records an audit of the measurements themselves. Two
findings dominate. First, the project's long-standing headline --- connectivity
scored against human annotation --- is \emph{not} a correctness result: its
specificity is exactly zero, because the annotation protocol can only record a
negative where the pipeline already proposed a link, so a true negative is
unrecordable. Second, two references that \emph{can} discriminate were
identified and scored: a 150-building generated corpus carrying 1{,}211 explicit
negatives, on which the method beats its strongest baseline at $p<10^{-6}$ at
every damage level, and an annotation-free flood-fill reference over the real
corpus which yields a real-corpus specificity of $0.644$ --- a number the
project had previously concluded was unobtainable, on the strength of an
argument that a later rebuild had already invalidated. The audit also corrects
eight figures that had gone stale in the repository's own documentation,
including the previous connectivity headline.
\end{abstract}

\noindent
\textbf{Generated corpus.} 150 buildings, 2{,}049 rooms, \textbf{1{,}708 positive
pairs and 1{,}211 explicit adjacent-but-not-connected negatives}. The negatives
are the design point: \code{main/generate\_building.py} exposes a
\code{door\_ratio} precisely so that some adjacent room pairs are \emph{not}
joined, because if every neighbouring pair has a door then naive adjacency
scores perfectly and the benchmark cannot separate any two methods.

\noindent
\textbf{Real corpus.} 25 models yielding 1{,}027 rooms (681 stated as
\code{IfcSpace}, 346 recovered geometrically). Of 146 storeys, only 74 contain
any \code{IfcSpace}; \code{IfcRelSpaceBoundary} --- the schema's own
connectivity relation --- occurs \textbf{zero} times across all 25 files, which
is what makes this a recovery problem rather than a translation problem.

\subsection{Two qualifications on the damage axis}

\textbf{The axis has two regimes, not four.} L2, L3 and L4 yield identical
pooled metrics for \code{adjacency}, \code{door\_near} and \code{ours}. The
files genuinely differ --- on one building \code{IfcRelAggregates} drops
$5\!\to\!4\!\to\!3$ and \code{IfcRelContained} $2\!\to\!0$ --- but the graphs
built from L2 and L3 are byte-identical for all three methods. Orphaning spaces
from their storey (L3) is absorbed entirely, because storey assignment is
recovered geometrically rather than read from containment; the undeclared
centimetre units at L4 are absorbed by scale detection. This is a robustness
result, but \textbf{no four-level severity gradient should be claimed for
connectivity}. What moves the number is the L1$\to$L2 step: missing
\code{IfcSpace}, where half the storeys lose their rooms and recovery must
supply them.

\textbf{\code{ifc2graph} collapses by construction, not on the merits.} It has
no room-recovery stage, so once L2 removes \code{IfcSpace} those rooms are
invisible to it, and at L3/L4 without spatial containment it emits nothing.
Claiming a win over a published external tool at L3/L4 would be hollow; its L1
figure ($F_1$ 0.738, specificity 0.000) is the fair one to quote.

Four qualifications, none of them optional:

\begin{enumerate}
  \item \textbf{Specificity is exactly zero: all 53 reference negatives are also
        edges the pipeline asserts.} This is a property of the protocol, not of
        the method. The reference's own metadata states its scope ---
        \emph{``links the pipeline proposed and a reviewer rejected''} --- so a
        rejection \emph{is}, by construction, one of the pipeline's own false
        positives, and a true negative cannot be recorded at all. No
        discriminative metric is computable from this reference.
  \item \textbf{Recall is a proposal-acceptance rate.} The reviewer only ever
        saw links the pipeline proposed; the reference's positives are the
        pipeline's own candidate set plus a handful of hand-added links. Room
        recall is worse still: all 375 reference rooms came from the pipeline
        and \textbf{zero} from an annotator, so room recall is 1.000 by
        construction and cannot detect a missed room at any rate.
  \item \textbf{A trivial baseline wins on precision.} Joining every pair the
        reviewer examined scores 0.918 against 0.876. With 53 negatives against
        387 positives, this slice is not discriminative.
  \item \textbf{83\% of judged items were swept in} with ``the rest of this
        floor is correct''. That is ground truth, but it was not inspected item
        by item.
\end{enumerate}

\noindent
\textbf{The edge-type figure deserves its own note.} It read 380/380 until
18 September 2026 and now reads 366/374. Composition copies the
\emph{pipeline's own} relation into the reference, so the metric compared the
pipeline to itself and could only ever read 1.000. Restoring one building's 8
missing doors changed the pipeline's relation for 8 pairs the frozen reference
still records as \code{open\_passage} --- and all 8 confusions are exactly
\code{open\_passage}$\to$\code{connected\_by\_door}. The metric therefore
measures neither correctness before nor after: it measures \emph{whether the
pipeline has changed since the reference was frozen}.

\noindent
\textbf{One building accounts for the degradation.} \code{model\_2} contributes
11 of the 13 false negatives and the single unmatched reference room, scoring
recall 0.780 where every other building scores 1.000 or 0.949. Its verdicts were
collected against the truncated file, whose missing doors had pushed its
connectivity onto the \code{open\_passage} mechanism. Re-judging the delta ---
3 rooms, 13 links, 1 floor link --- restores it.

producer.} Where a door has no authored \code{IfcOpeningElement} void, the

same projection-instead-of-section defect listed as an open method finding. It
follows that repairing A01 would improve the method \emph{and} the only
independent real-corpus reference in one change --- which argues for
prioritising it above A03--A05.

most actionable result in this document.

\bottomrule

\caption{Every measured error in the system falls in one relation.}
\end{table}

\noindent
Where IFC authors the answer --- a door product, a stair solid --- the
deterministic path is \emph{exact}. All 53 errors are in the one inferred
relation. Moreover \code{open\_passage} has no geometric signal in either
direction: the maximum inscribed-circle diameter of the free gap measures
$0.10$\,m for confirmed passages against $0.30$\,m for rejected ones on one
building, $0.21$ against $0.21$ on another, and $0.01$ against $0.00$ on a
third. It is measuring registration slop between \code{IfcSpace} footprints and
wall faces and calling it connectivity. Relatedly, the width test uses an
axis-aligned bounding box, which admits rejected passages with a median width of
$3.51$\,m and a maximum of $15.74$\,m --- a 15.74\,m ``doorway''.

\noindent
This is why the method should be \textbf{hybrid} rather than learned end to end:
nothing can improve on 1.000, and the learnable problem is confined to the
relation where geometry is silent.

\subsection{The VLM figure: fixed, not just bounded}

The 18 September 2026 run had two compounding defects. First, the generation
cap was 300 tokens, far below what this ``thinking'' model needs to close its
\code{<think>...</think>} trace before answering (a diagnostic probe over 24
rooms found median 85 tokens to close, p90 245, max 1004). Second --- found
while fixing the first, not reported by any prior measurement --- both
\code{VLMBackend.predict\_image} and \code{LLMBackend.predict} silently
substituted the rule backend's own hypothesis whenever a model's output failed
to parse (\code{\_coerce(text, fallback=hyp)}), so a non-answer scored as the
rule baseline's answer rather than as wrong. \textbf{95 of 592 rooms (16.0\%)}
hit this on the VLM path --- coincidentally almost exactly the model's own
margin over majority-class guessing, which is what made the original 0.505
figure look plausible while being unmeasurable.

Both are fixed: \code{max\_new\_tokens} raised to 1536, and both backends now
return \code{(label\_or\_None, status)} with
\code{status}~$\in\{$\code{ok}, \code{truncated}, \code{unparseable}$\}$ via a
new \code{\_coerce\_strict} that never substitutes a prior. The fix is
regression-guarded by \code{tests/test\_vlm\_answer\_accounting.py}, verified
to fail against the pre-fix code. Rerun 21 September 2026:
\textbf{590/592 answered (99.7\%), 2 truncated, 0 unparseable}. Accuracy over
answered rooms only is 0.498 --- essentially identical to the headline 0.497,
confirming the fix did not merely relocate the defect into a different
accounting bucket.

spatial reasoning problem rather than an OCR problem.

\noindent
\textbf{The pre-existing image corpus was unusable and every image had to be
replaced.} The previous renderer burned the answer into the pixels: room names
and areas in legible type, a \code{?} appended to every geometrically recovered
room, and a fill tint distinguishing IFC-stated from pipeline-recovered rooms
--- i.e.\ part of the ground-truth partition. Its \code{--no-labels} flag
suppressed only the first two. One ground-floor plan contained 949{,}601 pixels
of the recovered-room fill colour alone. The new renders contain at most 4 such
pixels, which is antialiasing coincidence; the leak gate is validated by being
run against the old images, where it fires.

\noindent
\textbf{Doors are now visually distinct from windows.} The previous renderer
placed \code{IfcDoor} and \code{IfcWindow} in one style bucket at identical
colour and weight, making door-mediated connectivity unrecoverable from vision
and the text-vs-vision comparison unfair --- the text modality receives the door
products explicitly.

\noindent
\textbf{The modality comparison is controlled, and this is measured.} All 150
generated buildings have byte-identical drawable geometry across L1--L4, and all
600 rendered images (300 plan, 300 occupancy) are \textbf{byte-identical across
all four damage levels}. \code{IfcWall} count is 8{,}912 at every level while
\code{IfcSpace} drops $2{,}049\to994$. The text modality therefore loses half
its rooms while the picture does not change by a single pixel, so any score
difference across levels in a vision condition cannot be attributed to the
image.

\noindent
\textbf{One stated limitation.} The occupancy raster is not bounded to the
storey's floor plate, because no \code{IfcSlab} survives a $1.2$\,m section cut.
Free space therefore continues past the building envelope wherever a real
opening exists, including through external doors. It is an image for a model to
read, not a segmentation mask.

returning ``no class'' both for a string carrying no function and for a real
function the taxonomy could not express --- and every caller discarded both
identically, so the taxonomy's blind spots were invisible. The sharpest
consequence: the generator emits \code{Lab} as one of twelve synthetic room
types and no keyword matched it, so \textbf{166 of 2{,}049 rooms in the primary
corpus (8.1\%) were silently unscorable}. That is now zero, and a regression
test fails if the generator gains a room type the vocabulary does not know.

Two resolution defects were also fixed: punctuation was never stripped, so
``W.C. (Women)'' did not match the key \code{wc}; and keyword matching took the
first hit in dictionary order, so ``Store Front'' resolved to \code{storage}
rather than \code{retail}. Longest-match was tried as a remedy and
\emph{rejected} --- it sends ``Lift Shaft'' to \code{service} rather than
\code{stair}, and a lift is vertical circulation. The ten genuine conflicts are
resolved by an explicit table instead.

\section{Reproducibility and determinism}
\label{sec:repro}

Running the pipeline's own single entry point a second time
(\code{./run\_pipeline.sh --regenerate 150}) reproduced all 20
(method $\times$ level) connectivity cells \textbf{identically at full
floating-point precision}, not merely to three decimals.

\textbf{What that does and does not show.} Checked against the archived corpus:
the 150 ground-truth files are \textbf{byte-identical 150/150}, while the 150
IFC files are \textbf{byte-identical 0/150} --- every input file differs in its
GUIDs and header, and the ground truth it encodes does not. So this
demonstrates that the parse $\to$ recover $\to$ score chain is invariant to IFC
identifier churn, which is worth having. It is \textbf{not} a fresh sample of
buildings and says nothing about variance across different layouts: the same
seed yields the same rooms by construction. A sampling-variance claim would
require varying the generator seed, which has not been run.

\subsection{Labelling measures the corpus, not the models}

Masked label accuracy is \textbf{0.066--0.130 across every model and every
modality}. The high text figures are transcription and collapse when the
architect's names are withheld: GLM-4-9B 0.791$\to$0.107, Qwen2.5-7B
0.875$\to$0.084, Qwen3-8B 0.952$\to$0.097. \code{synth150} contains only
\code{IfcWall}, \code{IfcDoor} and \code{IfcStair} --- no furniture and no
windows --- so a render carries almost no functional cue. \textbf{Vision
labelling must not be reported on this corpus.}

\section{What the evidence currently supports}

\begin{enumerate}
  \item \textbf{On a reference that can discriminate, the method wins
        decisively.} $F_1$ 0.998 and specificity 0.993 at L1, beating the
        strongest baseline by $+0.085$ with $p<10^{-6}$ and 131 wins to 0
        losses. Under the damage that matters --- missing \code{IfcSpace} ---
        it degrades to 0.899 and still wins at $p<10^{-6}$.
  \item \textbf{The real-corpus evaluation everyone had been quoting was the
        problem, not the method.} On the annotated reference the same contrast
        gives $p=0.805$ with the ablation ahead; on a reference with genuine
        negatives the ordering reverses. Specificity 0.000 versus 0.644 is the
        whole story.
  \item \textbf{Error is confined to one mechanism.} Door- and stair-mediated
        connectivity are exact (1.000, 1.000). All 53 measured errors are
        \code{open\_passage}, where the geometric signal is absent in both
        directions. This localises the research problem precisely and argues
        for a hybrid method rather than a learned one.
  \item \textbf{Repairing A01 pays twice} --- it is a defect in the method and
        in the only independent real-corpus reference simultaneously, which
        should lift it above A03--A05 in priority.
  \item \textbf{Of the learned results, exactly one is currently
        trustworthy}: the text LLM at 0.556 against a 0.346 floor and a 0.503
        rule baseline. The vision result is not usable until re-run.
\end{enumerate}
